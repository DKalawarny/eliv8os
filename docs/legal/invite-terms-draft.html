Operated by Sarlia Digital Inc., British Columbia, Canada · Effective [date paid plans open] · Version 1

The short version: send someone your link or code. They get half off their first paid month. If they keep paying, you get a thank you share of what they pay from their second month, for as long as they stay. Be honest that you get something, only invite people you know, and never sell it as a way to make money.

1.About these terms

These terms govern the Unstuck Map invite program (the "Program"), run by Sarlia Digital Inc. ("we", "us"). They add to the Unstuck Map Terms of Use and Privacy Policy, which also apply. If these terms and the Terms of Use conflict about the Program, these terms apply. By sharing your invite link or code, you agree to these terms.

2.Who can take part

  1. You must be 18 or older, have an Unstuck Map account in good standing, and live somewhere the Program is allowed. [Payouts are only available where our payment provider, Stripe, supports payouts to individuals.]
  2. You do not need to buy anything to take part or to earn a thank you share.
  3. Our employees, contractors and their households may take part, but may not earn a thank you share. [Confirm.]

3.Your link and code

  1. Each account gets one personal link and one code, and may make extra links for individual people. They are for your personal use and may not be sold, transferred or shared for others to use as their own.
  2. Someone counts as invited by you ("your Friend") when they create a new Unstuck Map account after opening your link, or enter your code within 30 days of creating their account, and have never had an Unstuck Map account before.
  3. Each person can be invited by only one person. If more than one link or code applies, the first one used counts.

4.How you may invite people

You agree to:

  1. Say that you get something. Whenever you share your link or code, make it clear that you may get a thank you share if the person pays. The message we suggest already does this. This is required by advertising and endorsement rules in Canada and the United States.
  2. Only contact people you know, or post in places where you are allowed to. Do not send bulk or unsolicited messages, buy contact lists, or message people who have asked you not to. Anti-spam laws, including Canada's anti-spam law (CASL), apply to you.

  5. Not pretend to be us, use our names or logos in a way that suggests you speak for us, or buy online ads that use our names or bid on them as search terms.
  6. Not invite yourself, make accounts for other people, or use fake or duplicate accounts.

5.What your Friend gets

  1. Your Friend's Unstuck Map plan is free, as it is for everyone.
  2. When your Friend first subscribes to the step by step help, their first paid month is half price. After that they pay the normal price, which they can see before they subscribe.
  3. The discount is for new customers only, applies once per person, and cannot be combined with any other offer unless we say so. It has no cash value.

6.Your thank you share

  1. For each Friend, you earn [20%] of each payment we actually receive from that Friend for the step by step help, starting with their second paid month, for as long as they keep paying.
  2. The share is worked out on the amount the Friend paid, not counting sales taxes (such as GST/HST), and in the currency they paid in.
  3. You earn nothing on: your own payments; payments made with the same payment card or payment account as yours; payments that are refunded, disputed or charged back; and anything paid by people your Friends invite. You only ever earn on the people you invite yourself.
  4. If a payment you earned a share on is later refunded or charged back, the share for it is removed, and may be taken from future shares.
  5. When a Friend stops paying, their shares stop. There is no limit on how many Friends you can invite, and no time limit on a Friend who keeps paying.

7.Getting paid

  1. Each share is held for 30 days after the Friend's payment, in case of a refund or chargeback, and then becomes payable.
  2. We pay once your payable shares reach $25 [CAD], through Stripe. You will need to set up a Stripe account and give Stripe the information it requires, including identity and tax details. Stripe's terms apply to that account.
  3. If a share was earned in another currency, it is converted once, when it is paid out, at the rate Stripe uses, which you will be shown.
  4. You are responsible for any taxes on what you receive. Where the law requires it, we will issue tax slips or forms and may ask for information to do so.
  5. If your payable shares stay under $25 for [24 months], or you close your account, [we will pay any payable amount of $5 or more / unpaid amounts under the minimum are forfeited].

8.When we can hold back or take away a share

We may hold back, reverse or cancel shares and discounts, and remove you from the Program, if we reasonably believe you have broken these terms, including through fraud, self-invites, fake or duplicate accounts, spam or misleading claims. Where we do, we will tell you why, unless the law or a fraud investigation prevents it, and you can ask us to review it.

9.Not a job or a partnership

The thank you share is a thank you for recommending Unstuck Map to people you know. Taking part does not make you our employee, agent, partner or representative, and you cannot make promises or agreements on our behalf.

10.Privacy

  3. We store no contact details for the people you invite. More is in our Privacy Policy.

Questions to bring to a lawyer

  1. Is this clearly outside the Competition Act's pyramid rules?Rewards are paid only on real sales to people who use the product, there is nothing to buy to take part, and there is one level only. Confirm that wording, section 6.3 in particular, is enough.
  2. Disclosure wording. Is "I get a small thank you if you stay" enough for Canada's Competition Bureau and the U.S. FTC endorsement rules, or must it say it is money?
  3. CASL. Users send the messages themselves, and we send none. Confirm we are not treated as the sender, and whether section 4.2 is enough.
  4. Tax. Do payments to Canadian users need T4A slips, and at what amount? What about U.S. users (1099 forms through Stripe)? Does any of this make users "small suppliers" for GST/HST?
  5. Payouts outside Canada. Is it simpler to start with Canada and the U.S. only?
  6. Quebec. Quebec requires French versions of terms and has stricter consumer rules. Should Quebec residents be excluded at first?
  7. The discount. Is "half off the first paid month" clear enough under consumer law, given the normal price is shown before checkout? Anything required about renewal?
  8. Forfeiting small balances (7.5) and changes (11). What is fair and enforceable?
  9. Limitation of liability (12.1). Is it enforceable as written for a consumer program?
  10. Unstuck Map's own Terms of Use. These terms rely on them, and they still need the same review.
